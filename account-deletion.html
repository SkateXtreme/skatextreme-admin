Delete Your Account – SkateXtreme

Last updated: 19 Nov 2025

If you would like to request deletion of your SkateXtreme account and associated data, please follow the steps below. You can request deletion even if you no longer have access to the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at info@skatextreme.com.sg from the same email address used in the app;

Include the subject “Account deletion” and the following details:

  • Your full name
  • Email used to sign in
  • Optional: Student names linked to your account

Identity verification

We verify requests using the email address on file. In some cases, we may request additional confirmation to protect your account from unauthorized deletion.

What data is deleted

  • Profile information (name, email, preferences)
  • Linked student profiles owned by you
  • Enrollments and attendance records associated with your account
  • Unpaid or pending payment intents created by you

What data we may retain

We may retain certain records where we have a legal or legitimate obligation, for example:

  • Invoices, receipts, and payment records for bookkeeping, fraud prevention, and tax compliance
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics that cannot identify you

Retention periods: financial records are typically retained for 5–7 years in accordance with applicable regulations.

Timeline

  • We acknowledge your request within 3 business days.
  • Deletion is completed within 30 days of verification (or sooner, where possible).

Effects of deletion

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You will lose access to your account and any remaining lesson credits.

Contact

For any questions, contact info@skatextreme.com.sg. See also our Privacy Policy.
