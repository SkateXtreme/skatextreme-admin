Support & Help

If you need assistance using SkateXtreme, you can reach our support team or connect with your assigned coach for personalized guidance.

Contact Support

Email us at info@skatextreme with your questions. Please include:

  • Your account username or registered email
  • Device & app version (see Settings → About)
  • Steps to reproduce (if reporting an issue)

If you work with a SkateXtreme coach, you can contact them directly for training-related questions.
